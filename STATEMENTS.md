## Notes on the map

- Lemma 4.5 (b): `cont_tail` assumes that the record of the top coordinate is not flagged forgotten. In the formalization a record is flagged forgotten exactly when its cell is the cell of the point 1, the last cell (`RecOK.forg_iff`), so the hypothesis says $i_m<I$, which (b) implies: a forgotten top coordinate placed in a cell below the last one is covered. When $i_m=I$ there is no cell above $C_{i_m}$, and `bound_le_integral` integrates over the cells $1,\dots,I$ only.
- Propositions 4.7 and B.3: the formalization proves their composition, the integer bound of Appendix B below the integral, as `bound_le_integral`; the comparisons of Proposition B.3 are the cell lemmas `cell2_div_le`, `chord_div_le` and `MC_le_aux`, used inside it.
- Theorem 4.3 and Corollary B.4: `relaxed_of_tables` and `le_v_of_tables` take integer tables and the integer bound as hypotheses.
- Proposition 2.4: `relaxed300` is stated with `RelaxedSubSolution` (Definition 2.1), defined in `Robbins/Relax/Defs.lean`. The theorems of Theorem 1.2 (`main_so`, `main_so_limit`, `two_lt_v`, `v300`) are stated with the definitions of `Robbins/Statement.lean` only.
- Proposition 2.4: the Lean modules of the tables and of the 4837 theorems that check them are not committed: `code/formal-proof/gen.sh` writes them under `RobbinsSO/N300/` from the output of `code/soq/run.sh`.
